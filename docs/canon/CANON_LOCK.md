# CANON_LOCK.md

Binding file. If another `/docs` file conflicts with this file, this file wins.

## Core premise

The **Liminal Union** is an ancient commonwealth south of the lands later called Wesnoth, across the Black River, with **Sayyon / Salem** as its civic heart. It is a threshold-civilisation shaped by freedom, law, social markets, public obligation, disciplined change, and the meeting of peoples, roads, and seas.

## Doctrinal lock

The Union is **anti-sorcerous in agency, not anti-arcane in substance**.

It forbids:
- direct spellcasting by mortal persons;
- mage-rule and occult sovereignty;
- hereditary magical privilege;
- mage-schools, grimoires, occult cults, and necromancy.

It permits:
- arcane matter, Arcanite, and reactive or medicinal alloys (**Pyrite**, **Kryonite**, **Uranite**, **Zoelite**, **Akosite**);
- lawful artifice, alchemy, metallurgy, and disciplined craft;
- bound cores, arcane wargear, and golem warforms governed by civic law.

Canon rule: **arcane force may dwell in lawful vessels, but must not rule through privileged persons.**

## Historical lock

The Union is already ancient before the rise of Wesnoth.

Its deep history includes:
- the **Sundering of the Halfelves** and the institution of the **Eighth-Day Severance**;
- the formation of **Sayyon**;
- the **Ban on Living Spellcraft**;
- the development of **Arcanite**, reactive alloys, and lawful anti-sorcerous craft;
- the **Treaty of the Living and the Bound** governing the vampiric border enclave.

The mature military order of the Union, including **G9 Warforms** and **Phoinix messengers**, predates the Green Isle arrivals and the **Scarlet Faerie Stone affair**.

## Biological and metallurgical lock

- **The Eighth-Day Severance**: When the Halfelven kindreds broke from the elder Elvish order, they severed their hereditary tie to the Faerie plane to live as mortal citizens under law. Because an unsealed break from the Faerie plane threatens to consume an Elvish-descended body, every Halfelven infant undergoes a chemical and metallic severance in the first eight days of life: seven days in a non-arcane threshold ward while receiving a dampening trace of **Zoelite** through the mother's milk (*smelting-milk*), followed on the eighth day by the **Catharite bath** in warm liquefied **Akosite** and an Akosite poultice upon the unclosed crown (fontanelle) to draw out residual faerie tethering. Though a small fraction of infants whose hearts are too deeply woven with faerie essence do not survive the shock, those who pass the eighth day are permanently anchored to the mortal plane and inscribed as full citizens of the Union.
- **Lawful Alloys**: Like Dwarvish rune-craft or Dunefolk alchemical naphtha, the Union's metallurgy binds elemental and vital properties into physical matter without living incantations:
  - **Pyrite**: a volatile, deep-mined reactive mineral grade that yields an intense thermal reaction on impact or under formation resonance (Fire).
  - **Kryonite**: an ultra-dense, heat-drinking deep-earth alloy that rapidly drains ambient warmth upon impact and braces defensive lines (Cold).
  - **Arcanite**: an alloy smelted with anti-sorcerous crystals bound directly into the metal matrix, disrupting the dark magic of the undead and the primal arcane vitality of draken (Arcane).
  - **Uranite**: a dense conductance mineral housed in officer rods and sealed vessels, steadying liminal soldiers against the harsh extremes of full Day and deep Night.
  - **Zoelite** and **Akosite**: companion medicinal alloys refined from deep forest-mountain iron ores long steeped in faerie-touched flora. Used in the Eighth-Day Severance and worn as passive metallic conduits that react to the vital warmth of **Phoinix Messengers** to draw poison from the wounded without living nature-magic.
## Founding character lock

Sayyon was built by those who came fleeing — peoples who had endured tyranny, hereditary oppression, resentment-rule, and the exclusion of the lowborn from law and learning. This origin is binding for the Union's character:

- the Union's foundational identity is the **open threshold**: those who arrive in need are received under law, not turned away by birth or condition;
- the **law of mutual obligation** is a pillar of the civic compact equal in standing to the Ban on Living Spellcraft: the stranger who arrives in need receives equal standing under law, as one would wish to receive if one oneself were fleeing; the former enemy who lays down arms and accepts the compact stands under the same civic protection, bound by the same obligations, held by the same courts — because the Union's founders were once refugees themselves, and they chose to build a commonwealth that would treat strangers as they would have wished to be treated;
- a **public fund of common provision** — relief for the destitute, support for the injured, shelter for the displaced — is not charity in the Union's law; it is a standing civic obligation, funded by public compact and inspected by law like any other civic institution;
- the lowborn are not a subject class; they are citizens with full claim on the compact, and the law holds no distinction of birth before it.

This is not to be framed as modern welfare-statism or ideological label. It is to be written as an old, severe, and unsentimental civic doctrine rooted in the Union's own history of flight and founding.

## Civic education lock

The Union regards universal education as a civic obligation equal in weight to lawful artifice and public service.

This includes:
- compulsory education for all children regardless of birth, kin, or wealth;
- the **Halls of Inquiry**: the oldest institutions of higher learning on the Great Continent, founded in Sayyon long before the rise of Wesnoth, open by merit rather than bloodline;
- an explicit doctrine that knowledge is civic property, not occult privilege — the direct counterpart to the Ban on Living Spellcraft;
- generations of universal public learning have produced a people of unusual general literacy, craft competence, and civic judgment.

The Union does not frame this as racial or bloodline superiority. It frames it as the long accumulated fruit of open institutions and public obligation.

## Military service lock

Military service in the Union is **universal civic obligation**, not feudal levy, mercenary contract, or hereditary duty.

- All citizens serve; rank is determined by merit, competence, and oath, not birth;
- the draft is a mark of civic belonging, not punishment or necessity alone;
- even scholars, artificers, and clerks carry public-defense obligation;
- this universal service reinforces the Union's identity as a commonwealth, not a caste order.
## Military lock

The Union’s armies are **disciplined public corps**, not feudal retinues.

Its four canonical military pillars are:
- **Maiden Corps** + **G9 Warforms**
- **Marine Corps** + **Phoinix messengers**

The Maidens are the Union’s precision and anti-sorcerous arm.  
The Marines hold coasts, crossings, ports, and defensive lines.  
The G9s are ancient warforms of artifice, not modern machines in tone.  
The Phoinixes are living emblems of renewal, transition, and memory, not divine-like.

## Diplomatic baselines

The **Crown-Realms of the Burning Standard** are lawful near-kindred, yet compromised by crown, sanctified power, and tolerated magi. They are rivals and occasional partners, not the absolute enemy.

The **Sun-Scar Necrodominion** is categorically different: a metaphysical affront to renewal, liminality, and civic life. Against necromantic realms, compromise is folly.

If the vampire covenant remains in use, vampires are to be treated as a corrupted but non-necromantic people tolerated only under strict law (**the Treaty of the Living and the Bound**, or *Dark Forest Charter*). Descended from occultist Elvish exiles who fled alongside the early Halfelves but refused mortal finitude — sustaining their faerie-severed bodies through blood-ritual harvested from borderland megafauna rather than turning to necromancy — they hold a recognized, strictly bounded buffer enclave in the Dark Forest against monarchist incursions from Evercrown. Under regulated compact, the Union supplies them with sealed containment vessels, filtration craft, and medical knowledge in exchange for border defense, condensed arcane-charged mineral residues from their rites (used in smelting volatile alloys), and stable bio-resonant cells that help power officer conductance vessels. Necromantic doctrine is never tolerated.
